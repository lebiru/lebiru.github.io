Classic Tower Defense, Built for Strategy

Build towers, manage your gold, upgrade your defenses, and survive increasingly dangerous waves. Every battlefield is different, and every tower has a purpose. Winning means finding the right combination—not simply building more.

Build Your Arsenal

Unlock specialized towers. Rain arrows from afar. Freeze formations. Shatter armor with artillery. Chain lightning through crowds. Manipulate enemies with gravity. Build the right defense for the battle ahead.

03

Command the Battlefield

You're more than an observer. Unleash powerful Bastion Skills to damage enemies, slow armies, strengthen towers, and turn desperate battles in your favor. Timing can decide the battle.

04

Fight an Evolving Army

Face tanks, healers, flying enemies, burrowers, elites, minibosses, and region-specific threats. Later waves combine enemy abilities to break strategies that worked before.

05

Explore 8 Fantasy Regions

Journey across Asterra, from Verdantreach's ancient forests and the frozen lands of Frostholm to the depths of Umbralum and fires of Aurelion. Every region brings new environments, enemies, characters, and challenges.

06

Conquer 48 Missions & 8 Bosses

Battle through 48 handcrafted campaign missions and confront a major boss at the end of every region. Bosses aren't simply enemies with more health. They're battles you have to solve.

07

Discover the Story of Asterra

Meet commanders, thieves, hunters, sailors, oracles, prisoners, and survivors fighting the invasion. Complete trials, unlock abilities, fill your Compendium, and uncover the mystery consuming Asterra.

08

Master New Game+

The campaign is only the beginning. Continue through NG+, NG++, NG+++, and NG++++ with stronger enemies, new strategic pressure, extra tower slots, Commander Relics, and exclusive rewards. How far can your Bastion survive?
